How to Switch to Tallow Haircare

What to expect in your first few washes, and how to give your scalp the time it needs to find its balance.

By the founders of Amun Organics

Switching to tallow haircare is less about a new product and more about letting your hair and scalp reset. Most conventional formulas rely on synthetic conditioners and strong cleansers that change how your hair behaves. When you move to a natural, nourishing routine, it can take a little time for everything to settle. Here is what to expect, and how to make the change feel easy.

Why there is an adjustment period

Many conventional products coat the hair with synthetic film-formers that make it feel smooth on the surface, while harsh cleansers strip away natural oils. Your scalp often responds by producing more oil to compensate. Our Regenerative Tallow Shampoo cleanses with gentle, coconut-derived cleansers instead, and grass-fed tallow nourishes rather than coats. As the old buildup lifts away and your scalp rebalances, your hair may feel different for a few washes before it feels better.

What to expect

  1. No. 001The first washes. Hair can feel heavier, waxier or less slippery than you are used to as residue from previous products comes away.
  2. No. 002The settling phase. Your scalp begins to adjust its oil production, and you may find you can go a little longer between washes.
  3. No. 003The new normal. Hair feels soft, light and full of natural shine, without needing a coating to get there.

Everyone’s hair is different, so the timeline varies with your hair type, how often you wash and what you were using before.

Your switching ritual

  1. No. 001Wash twice. While your hair adjusts, shampoo once to lift buildup, rinse, then shampoo again. Massage gently into the scalp in small circles.
  2. No. 002Condition the lengths. Smooth the Regenerative Tallow Conditioner through mid-lengths and ends, leave it for a few minutes, then rinse with cool or lukewarm water.
  3. No. 003Use less than you think. Tallow formulas are rich. Start with a small amount and add more only if you need it.
  4. No. 004Finish with the Leave-In. A little Leave-In Conditioner on damp or dry hair helps with softness, definition and frizz between washes.
  5. No. 005Treat once a week. Leave the conditioner on for about 15 minutes as a nourishing mask.

Give it a few weeks

Consistency matters more than perfection. Try to stay with your new routine for a few weeks before judging the results, and avoid switching back and forth with conventional products, which can bring the buildup back. If your hair still feels heavy, focus on rinsing thoroughly and using a smaller amount of product.
